Knowledge that works offline

The Know tab holds short survival guides (warmth, water, food safety, cooking, first aid, radio, hygiene). Written once from NHS, FSA and St John Ambulance guidance and stored on the device.

One thing a week

Prepare gives one small task at a time: buy, learn, plan or practise, with a UK price or time. Finishing a step moves you up to the next target, with no guilt streaks.

Profile makes it specific

Ten questions at setup: home type, heating, cooking, who lives there, medication. Steps such as "light the hob with a match" only appear if you have a gas hob. Everything stays on the device.
